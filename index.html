Student Enrolment Management System

A fictional multi-school rollout scenario used to showcase practical integration management: a clear baseline, transparent Amber escalation, evidence-based UAT and a controlled two-wave release.

The differentiator: a sample defect threatens UAT at W16. The project manager escalates, documents options, secures an approved contingency draw and requires objective retest before release.
